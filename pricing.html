Comprehensive Inspection

50 checkpoints. One flat price.

Every Autotech inspection follows the same depth of process whether the car is a daily Giulietta or a Quadrifoglio. We look at drivetrain, electrics, brakes, suspension, safety systems and the undercarriage. Every check below is performed and documented before the report leaves the workshop.

Inspection · all models
AED 350+ VAT
Flat fee. Same price across the full Alfa Romeo range, classics included.
Drivetrain & Fluids
  • Engine oil level & condition
  • Oil leaks around engine and sump
  • Coolant level & condition
  • Brake fluid level & contamination check
  • Power steering fluid level check
  • Windshield washer fluid check
  • Fuel leaks
  • Belt inspection
  • Engine hoses & pipes inspection
  • Engine mount inspection
  • CV boot inspection
  • Transmission leak check
Battery & Electrical
  • Battery voltage & health test
  • Battery corrosion check
  • Starter motor operation check
  • Exterior light function test
  • Interior light function test
  • Wiper & washer operation
  • OBD scan
Tires & Wheels
  • Tire inspection: tread depth, age, cracks, wear, pressure
  • Spare tire condition & pressure check
  • Wheel nut / bolt torque check
  • Wheel & rim damage inspection
Brakes
  • Brake pad thickness
  • Brake disc wear
  • Brake line condition
  • Brake fluid leak check
  • Parking brake operation
Suspension & Steering
  • Suspension bush inspection
  • Wheel bearing noise / play check
  • Ball joint play check
  • Steering rack leaks / play
  • Visual alignment check
  • Tie rod end inspection
  • Shock absorber leaks / wear
Safety & Undercarriage
  • Seat belt condition & locking
  • Door lock operation
  • Parking sensor functionality
  • Child lock functionality
  • Underbody damage / corrosion
  • Exhaust system leaks / rust check

Inspections are ideal before purchase, before a long trip, or when something feels off and you want a specialist’s eyes on it. You receive a written report on every checkpoint, with photos of anything that needs attention and a clear recommendation. Inspection price is flat across the full Alfa Romeo range, modern and classic.
